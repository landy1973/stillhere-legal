Our Commitment

StillHere was built around a simple idea: a quiet, daily signal that you are okay. We collect only what is strictly necessary to make that work. We do not sell your data. We do not show you ads. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the rights you hold under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR.

1. Data Controller

StillHere is the data controller responsible for your personal data. For all data protection matters, contact us at support@stillhere.app.

2. Personal Data We Collect

Phone Number
Required to create and verify your account via SMS. This is your account identifier.


Check-In History
The dates on which you check in, your current streak, and your longest streak. This is the core data the app exists to record.


Personal Info (Optional)
If you choose to fill in your profile — nickname, gender, blood type, home address, company, and a personal note — this is stored securely. You are never required to provide it.


Journal Entries (Optional)
Any text, voice transcripts, photos, videos, or files you save to your Journal are stored locally on your device and never uploaded to our servers.


Technical Data
Basic technical logs (app version, operating system) may be generated by Firebase for error reporting purposes. No advertising identifiers or tracking data are collected.

3. Special Category Data

Blood type constitutes health data under Article 9 GDPR, which is a special category of personal data requiring explicit consent. By entering your blood type, you give explicit consent to its processing solely for the purpose of storing it in your personal profile for your own reference. You may leave this field blank or remove it at any time.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases under Article 6 GDPR:

  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)) — processing your phone number and check-in data is necessary to provide the service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — enabling the administrator welfare review (to identify users who may need a welfare check) is a core purpose of the app that users reasonably expect.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a)) — optional profile fields (including blood type) are processed on the basis of your freely given, explicit consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

5. How We Use Your Data

We use your data exclusively to:

  • Verify your phone number and authenticate your account
  • Sync your check-in history and streaks across your devices
  • Send you daily local reminders that you personally configure
  • Allow the app administrator to see whether users have checked in recently, for welfare purposes
  • Maintain app security and diagnose technical errors

6. Data Storage and Security

Your account data is stored on Google Firebase (Realtime Database and Authentication), which operates data centres in compliance with GDPR. All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS 1.2 or higher). Journal entries, photos, videos, and files are stored exclusively on your device and never leave it. We retain your account data for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion at any time.

7. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, StillHere will send you a daily reminder at the time you choose. You can withdraw notification permission at any time in your device Settings. Withdrawing permission does not affect your account.

8. Third-Party Processors

We share data only with the following processors under data processing agreements:

  • Google Firebase Authentication — phone number verification via SMS OTP
  • Google Firebase Realtime Database — cloud storage and device sync

We do not use advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or any other third-party data processors. Google's privacy policy governs Firebase's data handling.

9. International Transfers

Google Firebase may store and process data in countries outside the European Economic Area. Google relies on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission as the legal mechanism for such transfers, providing an equivalent level of protection to that within the EEA.

10. Your Rights Under GDPR

As a data subject under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access (Art. 15) — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16) — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17) — request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restriction (Art. 18) — request that we limit how we process your data
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20) — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object (Art. 21) — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@stillhere.app. We will respond within 30 days.

11. Right to Lodge a Complaint

If you believe we have processed your personal data in breach of the GDPR, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. You may contact the data protection authority in your EU member state of residence, or — if you are in the United Kingdom — the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

12. Children's Privacy

StillHere is not directed at individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a minor has registered, please contact us and we will delete the account promptly.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. We will notify you of material changes through the app before they take effect. The "Last Updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

14. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, data subject requests, or concerns, email us at support@stillhere.app.
